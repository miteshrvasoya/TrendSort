# Workflow layer (v0.7.0)

## Weekly report

`gatherReportInput` (packages/db/src/services/report.ts) collects facts from stored scores (this week vs about a week ago), alerts, the pipeline and source health; `composeReport` (packages/shared/src/report.ts, pure and tested) builds the sections. Nothing is generated by an LLM and nothing is invented: suggested actions are rules over the data (missing margin scenario for a top-3 trend, missing reviews, import deadlines within 45 days, stuck ideas, stale or never-run sources, indicative duty/fee tables). Mode is `real` when you have scored live trends, otherwise `sample`, and sample reports say so at the top. One report is kept per week and mode.

## Alerts

Rules: `score_above` (Opportunity under your weights), `india_rising` (India's own momentum), `india_stage_up`. They fire on a crossing (previous run below the line, this run at or above it; or first sight above it) and are de-duplicated per alert, trend, rule and day. Scope "all" covers live trends only; trend and watchlist scopes cover whatever you chose.

## Pipeline

Spotted, Researching, Sourcing, Sample ordered, Listed, Scaling, Dropped. Every stage change is logged (`pipeline_events`). An idea untouched for 21 days (not Scaling or Dropped) is flagged stuck and appears in the weekly report.

## Prediction tracker

Rule (`predictStage`): `projected ratio = India share of foreign peak x (1 + quarterly growth)^(6/3)`, capped at 1.2, mapped to a stage with the same bands as the live stage. Scoring runs record one live prediction per trend per 30 days; once the horizon passes it is compared with the trend's latest stage. Reported: exact rate, within-one rate, mean absolute stage error, bias, accuracy by confidence band (calibration) and the accuracy of the **"nothing changes" baseline** (predict the stage seen at prediction time): the model has to beat that to be worth anything.
The **back-test** hides the last 26 weeks of each trend's own series, predicts from the rest and compares with what the full series shows. It is stored with origin `backtest` and never mixed with live results. On sample data it checks the machinery only.

## Analytics

Opportunity matrix (x foreign momentum, y India gap, size commercial proof or sustainability), country heatmap (momentum per country, top 15), rising stars (high foreign momentum, India Emerging or earlier), category treemap and marketplace movers (rank change between the last two snapshots).
